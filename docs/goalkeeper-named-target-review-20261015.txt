GOALKEEPER NAMED-TARGET COMPARISON AND AERIAL RECOVERY
15 October 2026

DECISION
Keep the live model and selection policy unchanged. Actual keeper targets are
better-defined research data, but the fixed-specification refit did not improve
probability accuracy. Its very high replay ROI comes from ONE winning bet and
does not justify promotion. No new candidate alerts or stakes were enabled.

RECOVERY
Read 2,049 retained/new raw files and recovered 2,019 distinct fixture records.
1,776 fixtures have two starting keepers, 90 minutes each, explicit saves agreeing
with attributable save events, and no target conflict. Other records are retained
with their conflicting or missing counts, not silently converted to zero.
42 missing completed evaluation matches were fetched by exact recorded fixture,
date and team IDs. All 42 succeeded. No paid API requests were used.

The full usable training join contains 2,778 goalkeeper rows from 1,389 fixtures,
15 August 2025 to 24 May 2026, across all five leagues. This replaces the earlier
118-row preparation. 350 labels differ from the old training proxy; named saves
total 7,840, against 8,023 proxy saves. This difference is data evidence, not ROI.

METHOD
Registered before fitting in registration.json. Both refits use exactly the same
training fixtures, nine existing features, NB2_FULL fitting method, regularisation
and dispersion bounds. Only the dependent variable differs: old team proxy versus
explicit named-keeper saves. The team_saves_ema20 feature remains the original
proxy in BOTH versions, isolating the target change. No feature, threshold,
surface/league filter, coefficient or date-range search was performed.

The historical count test trains before 1 March 2026 and evaluates March to May:
1,946 training keeper rows and 832 test rows, representing 416 held-out fixtures.
The frozen production model is not assessed on this historical holdout because
its own training overlaps that period. It is assessed on the later captured data.

LIMITS
The candidate was designed after historical outcomes existed. Chronological
splits prevent using later rows in fitting but do not make this prospective.
Training market inputs are historical prices and may represent a later time
than the early quotes used in the captured replay. The verified full-match
protocol excludes substitutions and disputed/missing counts, so coverage is not
random. Agreement within FotMob is not independent upstream-provider verification.
Probability scores average fixed thresholds 1.5 through 5.5, with uncertainty
resampled by whole fixture. Multiple thresholds are not independent observations.

AERIAL COLLECTION REPAIR
The extractor already read claims/punches; the storage allowlist dropped them.
The collector now retains goalkeeper identity flags, claims and punches, keeping
unknown as null and explicit zero as zero. Exact original source hashes and
player/team/minutes checks allowed historical backfill of 1,860 fixtures and
3,765 keeper appearances. 189 fixtures remain without that backfill: 31 have no
retained payload, 158 lack a matching original payload hash. No guesses or
additional fetches were made for these fields.
The existing public Aerial payload was byte-for-byte equivalent as a JSON object
at the same evaluation timestamp before and after the backfill.

The same existing match response now also retains named keeper saves/minutes/
starts and source evidence in data/aerial/keeper-history.json.gz. Initial recovery
contains 2,019 fixtures; source revisions are preserved separately. This avoids
limiting future outcome collection to matches with selected GK bets. Missing or
conflicting counts remain explicit. No extra requests, schedule, forecast inputs,
public feature, model weights or selection rules were introduced.
