## Contract checklist: all acceptable
- **Leakage:** forecast at most 6h old, quote at most 30 min old, quote ≤ observation ≤ now < start, report at most 120s old, and nothing before `enabled_at`. An older quote can't be reused once a book has a newer one.
- **Exact market and best price:** execution uses the receipt-bound raw 1X2 vector, with player order handled. Conflicting prices or kickoffs block the fixture. The bet takes the best-value outcome across fresh books.
- **Common reference:** the median of the books' margin-free probabilities, from at least 2 books with overround ≤ 1.2, frozen at capture. Both arms bet the same book and side, so P/L can only differ where the decision changed.
- **No-bets** are recorded in the paired cohort.
- **Dedup:** one record per model, edition, round and player-ID pair, so reschedules can't add a second bet. The ledger is append-only and written under a lock.
- **Settlement:** shared index, matched on edition, round and both names, with exactly one result required. Unclear results are held, never counted as losses. Settlement runs on copies, and each outcome is written once with hashes.
- **Null ROI** until the first settled bet (tested).
- **No new alerts or schedules.** Replay is excluded from the forward ledger, and earlier research results appear only as `seen_evidence`.

## Follow-ups (optional)
1. **Suspension gate.** `main()` skips new ledger registration when `shadow.most_aces_suspended()` is true (line 139), but it still runs `safe_capture`. The weekly Telegram text hides the other Most Aces lines while suspended (4416–4422), but not the new one. In practice the 6h forecast-age and 30-min quote limits make captures during a suspension unlikely. For a consistent lifecycle, skip `safe_capture` while suspended (recording status `SUSPENDED`) and apply the same check to the weekly line.
2. **Book coverage on first live run.** `compare()` only reads the `betmgm-` and `bet365-most-aces-1x2-{day}.csv` files (lines 135–136). A book only qualifies if its rows are also in the receipt's single `most_aces` artifact, with the same `capture_ts`. This fails safely, but check the first `capture-status.json` for `fewer_than_two_valid_books` to confirm Grosvenor and Sisal rows actually reach the lane.
3. **Exact `tour` match.** `decide()` compares raw `tour` exactly, while `validate_signal` normalises it first. If the inbox ever uses lowercase `atp`, every quote drops out silently, so compare with `norm()`.
4. **Calibration field in Monitor.** `monitor_rows` now puts `convention` (a string) and `reliability` (a list) inside `calibration`. If the dynamic renderer expects only scalars, the admin guard will log an error rather than crash. Check the first render.
