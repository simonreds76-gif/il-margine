### Card 2: ATP Tennis

**The Opportunity**
Tennis markets are deep for Grand Slams and ATP Masters events. But ATP 250s, ATP 500s, and Challenger tournaments exhibit persistent pricing inefficiencies.

**Why?**
- Lower betting volumes mean less market pressure to correct mispricing
- Bookmakers apply template pricing models rather than match-specific analysis
- Surface-specific performance differentials are systematically underweighted
- Tournament tier context (player motivation, fatigue) is poorly modeled

**The Edge**
Our proprietary framework incorporates:
- Surface-specific Elo ratings (not universal Elo)
- Rolling 12-month serve/return point win percentages by surface
- Tournament tier adjustments (motivation factors in 250s vs Masters)
- Fatigue modeling (consecutive weeks, travel distances)
- Head-to-head matchup specifics

**The Markets**
We focus on game handicaps and totals, not match odds. Why?

Match odds are efficient. The favourite is usually correctly priced.

Game handicaps and totals are inefficient. Bookmakers misprice the *margin* of victory.

**The Proof**
447 tracked bets. 54% win rate. +8.6% ROI.

### Process Card 3: Stake Sizing
**Risk Management**
- Base unit: 1 unit = recommended stake size
- Variable staking: 0.05u to 5.0u depending on edge confidence
- Singles only (no accumulators)

**Why singles?**
Accumulators amplify variance. Singles preserve edge over time.

**Example:**
- 5 selections at 2.00 odds, each with 55% true probability
- As singles: Expected profit
- As accumulator: Expected loss (due to bookmaker margin compounding)
