ISOLATED PUBLISHED ELO INPUTS — 30 OCTOBER 2026

Scope
The collector records published Tennis Abstract/Sackmann ATP and WTA Elo
snapshots. It does not compute a new rating algorithm, update player_elo,
call pricing models, select bets, settle records, or send Telegram messages.
The live pricing engine does not read data/tennis-elo-source.

Automation
Existing morning and nightly research tails call scripts/tennis_elo_source.py
after betting alerts. The on-demand refresh returns before this work.
One shared per-tour/UTC-day reservation and OS lock permit at most one HTTP
attempt per tour per day across these callers. Two public pages, no API key.
No new schedule. A 90-second process-tree timeout is followed by
--mark-interrupted, preserving the last good snapshot and daily reservation.
The next day can try normally. Routine lock contention returns SKIPPED.
An abruptly powered-off PC may retain RUNNING until the next invocation;
the OS lock releases on exit and next-day collection is not blocked by it.

Evidence and validation
data/tennis-elo-source/{atp,wta}/status.json records latest state and last_good.
raw/, parsed/, snapshots/ contain hash-addressed immutable evidence. Only
status changes in place. Snapshot availability starts at observed_at,
including new identity/parser versions; the source's update date alone is
not a verified pre-match availability time. No historical backdating.

Complete HTML/table, contiguous ranks, required columns, sane rating ranges,
unique profile keys, row floors and prior-coverage checks must pass. Future,
regressed or >14-day-old source dates fail. Failures retain last_good;
snapshot_current independently rejects stale/future/hash-invalid evidence.

Identity mapping requires unique exact full names or explicit aliases,
corroborated by matching native and Sackmann birthdate/country. No initials
or surname-only guessing. Unknown fields in duplicate archive rows are not
contradictions; those archive IDs remain unverified and are never merged.
Known conflicting identities, source homonyms and competing native-ID claims
are quarantined with reasons. This is an Elo map, not a history-merging rule.

hElo/cElo/gElo are already blended overall/surface ratings. They are typed as
surface_blended, never raw surface. I.hard uses a hard-blend proxy in the
offline diagnostic only; the source publishes no distinct indoor rating.
No inversion of rounded blended ratings is attempted.

Retention
128 MiB ceiling per tour, warning above 80%; no automatic evidence deletion.
Unchanged source/mapping/parser retains its original observation. Raw and
parsed evidence are deduplicated by hash. On warning, archive old immutable
files by hash to separate cold storage, verify recoverability and keep the
last-good snapshot plus referenced raw/parsed artifacts accessible before
removing local copies. This archival step is not automated or authorised
by the collector. Capacity exhaustion is a visible failure, not silent loss.

Validation / interpretation
28 regression tests plus real process-timeout recovery were verified.
The 30 October offline full-engine diagnostic reproduces 47 saved baseline
forecasts; 44 have both players in the validated source. Rune, Demin and
Dominko are not on that edition's page. All arms use identical saved inputs,
apart from the stated Elo substitution / in-memory surface interpretation.
Recent-activity opponent-strength aggregates remain frozen. This measures
partial direct sensitivity, neither a lower bound nor full refresh impact.
Current ratings must not be applied retrospectively to claim backtest ROI.

Live formulas, ledgers and alerts remain unchanged. Further evaluation needs
genuinely pre-match ratings/inputs/odds and identical settled fixture cohorts.
Today's price movements do not establish improved accuracy or profitability.
