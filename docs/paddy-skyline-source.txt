Paddy / Skyline source integration, 28 October 2026

Scope: a price and settlement adapter, not a new probability model. Frozen
Skyline artifacts, thresholds, 1u paper stakes, league coverage and existing
record keys remain unchanged. No headed-goal selections are generated.

The existing aerial publisher requests exact Paddy competitions for currently
upcoming supported fixtures, then batches the two explicit header market types.
Maximum eight requests / 55-second start budget; no 403/429 retries. Failed
captures keep their original quote timestamp, successful withdrawals clear old
quotes, and the existing two-hour price limit still applies. Private raw latest
files stay out of website deployment bundles. Individual runner evidence and
latest quotes are retained in the existing private Skyline snapshot.

Fixtures require football event type, correct competition, oriented club aliases
and exact kickoff. Players require a unique full-name match in the current XI.
Unmapped players remain quote research. Only confirmed XIs enter the ledger.
The highest current eligible headed-SOT price is selected. The original record
key excludes bookmaker, preventing another alert on a later source/price change.

Old records keep named-starter own-stat settlement. New Paddy records use its
participation rule, plus Super Sub for EPL under the rules checked 28 October.
The promotion rules must be reviewed before 1 July 2027. Extra time is excluded.
Replacement chains use explicit completed-match substitution pairs; missing or
ambiguous evidence remains pending. A named-player hit can settle independently
of missing substitution evidence. A nonstarter who later plays remains a runner
under Paddy's rule, but does not enter named-starter calibration evaluation.

Model probabilities and selection EV retain the named-player event and receive
no invented promotion uplift. Bookmaker-contract ROI and named-player Brier/log
loss use different outcomes where a replacement hits. Contract-specific stats
are also retained; historical results are never regraded onto the new contract.
The source is statistical evidence, not a bookmaker settlement receipt; Opta
replacement attribution and source corrections can require manual review.
