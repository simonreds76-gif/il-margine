Published Elo full-input research comparison -- 31 October 2026

This arm runs inside the existing bounded Elo Rebuild paper comparison after
daily signals. It uses the existing settlement, private snapshot and weekly
review paths. No new schedule, Telegram betting alerts or live pricing writes.

The control is the actual current full model. The research arm substitutes the
validated published overall/surface ratings AND recomputes recent opponent Elo
averages. Everything else uses the same formulas and inputs. All changed in-memory
inputs are restored. Missing player/opponent ratings or non-reproducible activity
exclude a pair; never use 1500 defaults or mix rating sources in paired evidence.

Published surface ratings already contain an overall/surface blend. The incumbent
writer's semantics remain unverified. This is a source-replacement experiment,
not a clean test of rating freshness alone. WTA is not included in this arm.

Only predictions calculated before verified kickoff with an already observed
source snapshot and an existing Pinnacle quote at most two hours old enter the
forward ledger. Quotes are normalized to player order. There is no extra odds
download. Old snapshot dates do not establish historical availability. Offline
sensitivity results must never be called prospective results or betting ROI.

Storage: data/tennis-elo-rebuild/published-source. Frozen forecasts are append-only;
outcomes are separate. Source, engine, policy and handicap calibration hashes are
registered. A changed dependency blocks further capture for manual review.
Exact native edition, both player IDs, round and saved kickoff date are required
to settle. Retirements/walkovers are research voids. Date ambiguity is explicit;
no silent nearest-date or qualifying/main-draw joins. Changed kickoff evidence
requires a separate audited amendment, not an edited original forecast.

Report incumbent-all, incumbent-paired and published-paired separately, ATP and
Challenger separately, and by surface. Primary accuracy is paired log loss and
Brier, with descriptive edition-clustered uncertainty. Handicap calibration uses
the identical common priced, live-gate-valid non-push fixtures in both arms.
Production H0 pair/fallback, correction, sign-flip and market gates are retained;
BO5 handicaps and quarter-line bets are excluded. Pushes refund paper stakes.

Primary paper ROI uses the available Strict hard-court/Masters gates. It is a
hypothetical reconstruction, not an actual issued-bet record (injury/editorial
overlays are unavailable). Broad EV>=5% is separately labelled diagnostic. All
paper bets are flat 1u; ROI uses settled win/loss stakes, excluding pending,
voids and pushes. Zero real stake. Neither arm may be automatically promoted.

Review at 300 independently settled paired fixtures AND ten tournament editions,
separately by tier. Challenger published-rating coverage is sparse and may take
months. Existing K22 Challenger stop remains unchanged; this separate published
source arm does not revive that rejected candidate. Existing top-level K22
review gates can stop the shared collector for manual review.

Validated by regression checks for dependency recomputation/restoration, missing
ratings, reversed quotes, stale/future prices, started matches, immutable capture,
duplicate prevention, exact settlement, push/void ROI and stale-context fail-fast.
The normal pipeline refreshes live context; research only validates it and stops
if stale. It never repairs live inputs as a side effect of a comparison.
