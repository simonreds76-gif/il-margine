## SECTION 3: WHAT TO EXPECT

### Heading
**Realistic Expectations**

### Content

**Variance is real.**  
Winning months and losing months happen. Even with positive long-term edge, short-term results fluctuate. This is normal.

**Sample size matters.**  
Performance over 20 bets is mostly noise. Over 100 bets, signal emerges. Over 200 bets, edge becomes clear. Judge long term, not week to week.

**ROI varies by market.**  
Player props typically higher ROI than tennis (wider margins, more exploitable). Match odds lower ROI (tighter margins). Staking adapts accordingly.

**Account restrictions affect access.**  
If you follow picks successfully, bookmakers will limit your stakes eventually. This impacts your ability to replicate exact results, but it's proof the approach works.

**Losses get logged.**  
Every selection gets recorded, winner or loser. Transparency over marketing.
