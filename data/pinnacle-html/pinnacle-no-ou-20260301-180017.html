Tennis betting and tennis odds

Tennis is a global game played in either a Singles, Doubles, Mixed Doubles or Wheelchair format with seasons following a yearly schedule which culminates in the season-ending ATP and WTA Finals in November. This creates a myriad of opportunities to bet on the sport with Pinnacle. Things to consider include player form and performances, their recent wins and losses and performance on specific surfaces.

How to bet on tennis

You should broaden your horizons when betting on tennis, as throughout both the WTA and ATP Tour circuits there are a large number of events other than the four Majors every season. There may be an edge to find on an ATP/WTA 250 or 500 event, whilst Challenger events give players both new and old an opportunity to rise up the rankings and secure spots in the larger 1000 events or Majors.

Types of tennis bets and tennis betting lines

At Pinnacle, you can find all the markets, lines and odds that make up tennis betting. You can place bets on everything from the Challenger events to the Grand Slams and Singles, Doubles and Mixed Doubles formats.

Tennis is either played as best-of-three sets or best-of-five and as such this can alter your perspective when betting. Here’s a round-up of the many ways you can bet on the game.

Tennis set handicaps

Tennis set Handicap betting involves wagering on the difference in the number of sets won between two players. Here’s how it typically works:

Let’s say Player A is facing Player B in a tennis match. A sportsbook may offer a set handicap like:

Player A -1.5 Sets (+1.5 sets)

Player B +1.5 Sets (-1.5 sets)

If you bet on Player A with a -1.5 set handicap, they need to win the match by a margin of at least two sets for the bet to be successful. If your bet is placed on Player B with a +1.5 handicap, they can win if they either win the match or lose by a maximum of one set.

Tennis game handicaps

Tennis game Handicap betting involves wagering on the number of games one player will win compared to their opponent during a match. Here's how it generally works:

Let's say Player A is facing Player B in a tennis match. The sportsbook might offer a game handicap like:

Player A -3.5 Games (+3.5 games) Player B +3.5 Games (-3.5 games)

If you bet on Player A with a -3.5 game handicap, they need to win the match while winning at least four games more than Player B for your bet to win. If you bet on Player B with a +3.5 game handicap, they can win the bet if they either win the match or lose by fewer than four games.

Tennis Total Games

Tennis Total Games betting involves predicting the total number of games played in a match, regardless of the match outcome. Here’s how it generally works:

Sportsbooks set a line representing the total number of games they expect to be played in a match. For example:

Over 21.5 Games Under 21.5 Games

If you bet on the \"Over 21.5 Games\" option, you win if the total number of games played in the match is 22 or more. If you bet on the \"Under 21.5 Games\", you win if the total number of games played is 21 or less.

Tennis Over/Under

Tennis Over/Under betting involves predicting whether a specific statistic relating to a tennis match will be higher or lower than a predetermined value set by the sportsbook. The most common and popular types of Over/Under bets in tennis are:

Total Games Over/Under

: This involves predicting whether the total number of games played in a match will be higher or lower than a specified number set by the sportsbook. For example, Over 21.5 Games or Under 21.5 Games.

Total Sets Over/Under

: Here, you predict whether the total number of sets played in a match will be more or fewer than a set value determined by the sportsbook. For instance, Over 2.5 Sets or Under 2.5 Sets.

Tennis Prop bets

On some matches, Proposition bets (Prop bets) can be offered by a bookmaker. These are bets on the specific performance of a player in a match.

Live tennis betting - In-play tennis betting

In-play tennis betting, also known as live betting, allows you to place wagers on a tennis match while it's in progress. It's an exciting form of betting that lets you react to the changing dynamics of the game. Real-time odds fluctuate depending on the momentum and scoreline of the match, allowing you to bet on the winner of the set, total games within that set and more. It requires a combination of fast and decisive decision making, whilst knowing when to hedge and cash out your bets at the right time to mitigate losses.

What are the four tennis Majors and when do they take place?

US Open

The US Open is a hard court tennis tournament played at Flushing Meadows in Queens, New York every year during late August and early September. Chronologically, this means the event is the final Major of the year for the competitors.

The US Open has a rich history dating back to 1881. It has seen legendary matches and iconic players gracing its courts. The tournament consists of five main event championships: Men's Singles, Women's Singles, Men's Doubles, Women's Doubles and Mixed Doubles. The Wheelchair tournament was postponed in 2024 due to clashes with the 2024 Paralympic Games in Paris. The main draws for Singles events include 128 players, while Doubles events include 64 pairs. The tournament follows a single-elimination format.

Wimbledon

The Wimbledon Championships that take place in London, England are unique as they are the only grass court major championships on the tour. Wimbledon is played as the third Major of the season during the first two weeks of July. The tournament is the oldest tennis tournament in the world, founded in 1877, and for many it is the most prestigious on tour.

The grass surface at Wimbledon poses challenges and demands a particular style of play, favoring players with strong serves and net games.

French Open

The French Open at Roland-Garros, Paris was founded in 1891, and has been played on many different surfaces in its illustrious history. It started on grass courts before moving to sand covered courts. From 1908 it has been played on clay courts.

The tournament is played in late May and early June, making it the second major championship in the calendar. The clay courts represent a unique challenge for players. They require different tactics and playing styles, favoring players with strong baseline games and defensive skills.

Australian Open

The Australian Open tennis tournament is played in Melbourne, Australia, and is commonly referred to as the ‘Happy Slam’ due to it being the first Major of the calendar. It serves as a fresh opportunity for players to dream of big achievements at the beginning of the season.

The tournament started in 1905, but it wasn’t designed to become a major championship until 1924 and has been played in Melbourne since 1927. It was originally on grass courts until a 1988 switch to outdoor hard courts, which means there are now two hard court Grand Slam tournaments on the tour.

How are tennis odds determined and calculated? How to read tennis odds

Tennis betting odds are determined by various factors, but they are primarily based on the probability of different outcomes of a match. Bookmakers use a combination of statistical analysis, historical data, expert opinions and market demand to set odds. Here's a simplified view of how odds are determined and calculated:

Player rankings and form

: Bookmakers consider player rankings and recent form as a starting point. Players with higher rankings or better recent performances might be favored, which can affect the odds.

Head-to-head records

: Historical head-to-head records between players are crucial. A player might have a better track record against a particular opponent, which can influence the odds.

Injuries and conditions

: Online information about player injuries or fitness issues can significantly impact the odds. A player returning from an injury might have higher odds against them.

Surface and playing conditions

: Different players perform differently on different surfaces. Odds might shift based on whether the match is on clay, grass or hardcourt.

Market demand and balancing

: Bookmakers consider the betting market demand. If a significant number of bets are placed on one player, they might adjust odds to balance their liabilities.

The three main types of odds formats are Decimal odds, Fractional odds and American odds.

Tennis betting tips

Reviewing the history between the sets of players, you might find that some players perform exceptionally well against specific opponents regardless of their current form. As previously mentioned, the differing surfaces and conditions can have a major effect on player performance and on live betting odds, which can often lead to the odds fluctuating quickly. Knowing when to adjust your strategy could be vital.
