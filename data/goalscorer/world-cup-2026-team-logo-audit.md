# World Cup 2026 Team Logo Audit

Audit date: 2026-05-21

## Verdict

The current assets are good enough as national-team visual marks, but they should not be described internally as fully verified official federation media assets yet. They came from a third-party logo library and were cross-checked against Wikipedia/team-logo references plus targeted source checks for the obvious edge cases.

No asset currently looks like a random or unrelated logo. The main caveat is precision: several rows are federation crest variants or national-team badge shorthand rather than direct federation corporate logos.

## Summary

- Keep - strong/plausible: 31
- Keep - team badge, not federation logo: 3
- Keep visually - verify exact variant: 1
- Keep for now - needs official media check: 13

## Important Edge Cases

- Germany: keep the eagle/four-star crest concept. The 2025/26 green DFB corporate rebrand does not replace the national-team eagle crest. The shipped all-gold badge is still an exact-variant check, not a certified federation master asset.
- New Zealand: the fern is intentional. NZ Football changed its corporate logo in 2022, but the national teams kept the fern leaf.
- Switzerland and Turkiye: these are national-team flag/roundel badge treatments, not federation corporate logos. That is acceptable for a country-team page, but do not call them federation logos.
- Rows marked weak reference should be rechecked against federation media assets before any future copy says "official".
