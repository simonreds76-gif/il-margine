What does this actually tell me?

Follow a manager across clubs

A manager’s record includes only fixtures where they were named as the coach on that match’s record. Changing clubs doesn’t reset the history.

Head-to-head means those two managers

Choose an opposing manager to retain their meetings, across any clubs in the archive. Leave it blank to include all opponents.

Winning matches isn’t the same as value

A win at 1.50 earns 0.50u; losing costs 1u. Ten wins can still lose money if the prices are short enough. ROI accounts for the recorded prices.

Coverage, exclusions and limits

Five domestic leagues only, from 2012/13 through the last available 2025/26 fixture. Cups and European competitions are excluded. This preview joins match-specific manager records to Football Atlas by league, date, both clubs and final score. Conflicting results, ambiguous managers and missing prices are excluded.

Historical Pinnacle closing or labelled last pre-match prices are retained. No double-chance or handicap prices are invented. Small H2H samples and a few large winners can dominate returns. Historical ROI is descriptive, not evidence that a manager causes a betting edge.
