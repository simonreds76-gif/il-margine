What the numbers mean

Aces & double faults

See the total recorded and the average per match. For example, 30 aces in 5 matches means 6 per match. Longer matches give players more chances, so we also show the count per 100 service points.

The chance suggested by the odds

This percentage comes from the recorded pre-match prices, with the bookmaker’s margin removed. A 60% chance means roughly 6 wins in 10 similar matches. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Excess wins & win-frequency gap

8 wins when the odds suggested 6 means +2 excess wins. Across 10 matches, that is an 80% win rate instead of 60%: a +20 percentage-point win-frequency gap. Neither number is betting profit or a prediction.
